About

A developer who ships, not just codes.

I'm a Software Engineering student at 1337 Coding School (42 Network — UM6P) in Rabat, following a project-based, peer-to-peer curriculum — no lectures, just problems, code reviews, and relentless debugging alongside other students.

Before that, I earned a Higher Technician Diploma in Computer Science from EST, graduating with top marks after building a full e-commerce platform solo — from database design to the admin dashboard.

Most recently, I completed a full-stack internship at Ejahiz, a web and digital marketing agency, where I shipped three production websites — including a bus ticketing platform with real payment integration — on real client deadlines instead of academic ones.

I like the parts of engineering most people skip: reading someone else's code before touching it, modeling a system with UML before writing a line, and making sure the seat selection screen still works on a phone with a cracked screen. I'm looking for an internship or job — remote or on-site, I'm happy to relocate — where I can keep doing exactly that, alongside engineers who'll show me what I don't know yet.
